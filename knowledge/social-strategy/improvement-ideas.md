# Ideas to do better on social (Feeling Yachty)

These are recommendations from **Nala** (reports to Pac-Man), using today’s briefing, the LinkedIn baseline (85 followers, 29 impressions / 30 days, 0 comments), and the brand’s real inventory. Site or spend moves go to Pac-Man as cards. Do not publish from this list.

## Fix the operating system first

1. **Answer the LinkedIn inbox.** Three aging threads (Upwork, jet-ski referral, job applicant) make the brand look unmanned. Social cannot out-create an ignored inbox.
2. **One Slack ritual, two products.** Keep `#social-media-post-communications` for “what posted / what inbound.” Put performance (impressions, holds, bookings) in the dashboard briefing, not only in chat.
3. **Define the conversion.** Every channel has one primary action: WhatsApp or quote form. Followers are a side effect.
4. **Ship Pixel + CAPI + GA4 key events** before any “big ads launch.” Otherwise we will scale noise.

## Content pillars (80% proof, 20% personality)

1. **Boat proof** — specific yacht, guest count, marina, 15 seconds
2. **Occasion** — birthday, bachelor, wedding, corporate, proposal, memorial
3. **Operator trust** — Captain Alberto, Karin, Fernando, safety, timing
4. **City** — skyline, sandbar, San Blas, event-week survival guides
5. **Offer** — “this weekend,” “Art Basel week,” “NYE buyout”
6. **Funny / light** — allowed, never the whole calendar

The Drive blog clusters (Miami + Panama) are a caption and SEO engine. Each published article should become: 1 Reel hook, 1 carousel, 1 LinkedIn text post, 1 RSA headline pack.

## LinkedIn recovery (this is the weakest owned surface)

- Post Tuesday / Wednesday / Thursday at Bogota morning desk time (you already check 10:00 and 16:00).
- Formats: 150–250 word operator story, one photo of a real charter, one CTA.
- Topics: corporate charter checklist, what $X actually includes, event-week marina reality, Panama vs Miami for a company offsite.
- Employee advocacy: Fernando and Karin share as people. Company pages without people do not move.
- First comment on every post: guest range + “message us dates.”
- Do not post blog dumps with no point of view.

## Creative production

- One half-day shoot = 12 ad variants, not one hero film
- Burned-in captions always
- No stock yachts
- Safety + luxury in the same week so corporate buyers are not only seeing bottle service

## Measurement you should see on this dash every week

- Reels: 3s rate, saves, shares, profile visits
- Site: yacht views → quotes → booked (GA4)
- Ads: cost / qualified lead, cost / booking
- Inbox SLA: time to first response
- GSC: top 20 queries, CTR on money pages
