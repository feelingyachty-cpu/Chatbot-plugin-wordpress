# Creative and placements

## Placement reality

Advantage+ placements should stay on for performance campaigns. Meta will spend into:

- Instagram Reels, Feed, Stories, Explore, profile
- Facebook Reels, Feed, Stories, Marketplace, in-stream
- Messenger
- Audience Network (on by default in Advantage+; watch quality — exclude if you see junk inventory)
- WhatsApp and Threads when eligible

Organic and paid creative must be **Reels-native**: 9:16, first 2 seconds without logo soup, captions burned in, sound on.

## Formats that work for yacht charter

| Format | When |
| --- | --- |
| Vertical Reel 15–30s | Default prospecting. Captain, wake, skyline, toast, cut to CTA |
| Vertical Reel 7–12s | Retargeting and Advantage+ creative variants |
| Carousel | 3–5 yachts or “birthday / corporate / sunset / wedding” occasions |
| Still + motion (Ken Burns) | Only if the still is extraordinary. Luxury can do stills; most stills die in Reels inventory |
| Collection / catalog | When the yacht catalog is clean enough to show price + image |
| Partnership ad | Creator or captain-facing handle. Highest trust for holiday 2026 per Meta |

## Creative system (not one-off posts)

Every concept needs:

1. **Hook** (0–2s) — a specific image or line. “49 people. One 60ft cat. Downtown Miami behind them.”
2. **Proof** — real boat, real water, real crew. No stock sunset that could be Cabo.
3. **Offer** — what they can book (sunset 2 hours, full day, wedding buyout).
4. **Friction killer** — captain + crew included, BYOB rules if true, instant quote.
5. **CTA** — “Get dates and pricing” / “WhatsApp the desk” / “See this yacht.”

Produce in batches of 8–12 variants from the same shoot. Meta needs volume. Feeling Yachty already has yacht photography and page copy — turn listings into ads, do not invent a second brand.

## Copy rules

- 3–5 primary texts, 3–5 headlines, 1–2 descriptions.
- Lead with the occasion or the constraint: “Bachelor party Saturday in Miami” beats “Luxury yachting redefined.”
- Put the real limiter in the ad: guest count, marina, season, holiday weekend minimums.
- Do not claim “cheapest” and “110ft Hargrave gala” in the same ad.
- Landing page must match the ad. Sunset ad → sunset / itinerary page, not the homepage.

## Advantage+ creative

Leave on for crops and text tests. Review:

- No auto-music that feels like a beach-bar template on a $20k wedding
- No safe-zone text covering the yacht name
- No low-quality zoom on faces of guests without model/release confidence

## Brand safety for Feeling Yachty

- Show crew professionalism and safety gear in at least some ads. LinkedIn and corporate buyers are watching.
- Alcohol is fine; intoxication and unsafe swimming are not.
- Do not imply celebrities will be there.
- User-generated content is gold if we have permission.

## Organic vs paid

Boosting a random funny post is allowed as a *test*, not a media plan. Paid should use Ads Manager with a conversion event. Organic Reels still matter because they seed custom audiences (viewers, engagers) that paid then scales.
