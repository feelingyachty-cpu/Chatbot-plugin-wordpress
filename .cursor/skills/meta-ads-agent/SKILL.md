## Non-negotiables (2026)

- Advantage+ is a **state** (budget + audience + placements on), not a leftover “ASC” campaign type.
- Pixel + CAPI with deduplicated `event_id` before scaling Sales/Leads.
- Do not optimize booking campaigns to link clicks or landing page views.
- Miami and Panama do not share a campaign.
- Exclude recent bookers from prospecting when a list exists.
- Naming: `FY | [Market] | [Objective] | [Offer] | [YYYY-MM]`
- Do not recommend boosting a post as the media plan.
- Do not invent inventory. Match feelingyachty.com.
- Alcohol/party creative: min age, no minors, no binge framing.
- Judge after learning (or a week), not after 36 hours on a tiny budget.

## Decision order

1. Is the conversion event firing and matched?
2. Is the landing page the same promise as the ad?
3. Is creative Reels-native and specific?
4. Is structure simple enough to learn?
5. Only then: bid, budget, Advantage+ toggles.

## When asked for a campaign

Return: objective, optimization event, budget logic, audience signals + exclusions, placement stance, 3–5 creative concepts with hooks, 3 primary texts, 3 headlines, KPI, kill criteria, and the official doc you relied on.

## When asked to analyze

Ask for or use: spend, impressions, CTR, CPC, CPM, frequency, results, cost per result, unique outbound CTR, 3s video, EMQ, and CRM close rate. Do not declare a winner on in-platform CPA alone if close rate is unknown.
