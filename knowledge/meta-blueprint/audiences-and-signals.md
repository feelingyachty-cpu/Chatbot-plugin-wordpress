# Audiences and signals

Official: [Meta Business Help — audiences](https://www.facebook.com/business/help)

## Four audience families

### 1. Advantage+ audience (default)

You give Meta *suggestions*. It can expand beyond them. This is the 2026 default for Sales and Leads.

Good suggestions for Feeling Yachty:

- Website visitors (180 days) and quote-form openers (30 / 90 days)
- Video viewers of yacht Reels (50% / 75%)
- Engagers of the IG/FB professional accounts (90 days)
- Customer list of past bookers (hashed email / phone)
- 1% lookalike of bookers or high-value deposits, US and Panama separately
- Interest seeds only as hints: yacht charter, Miami nightlife, luxury travel, destination weddings, corporate events — never as the only fence

### 2. Custom audiences

Built from your data:

| Source | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Pixel / CAPI events | ViewContent on yacht pages, Lead, Schedule, Purchase |
| Customer list | Past bookers, high-LTV, no-shows, unsubscribes |
| Catalog activity | Viewed a specific yacht, abandoned inquiry |
| Engagement | IG/FB/Reels/lead form engagers |
| App (if any) | Not in play |

Refresh lists. A 2019 email dump with 40% bounce rate poisons match rate.

### 3. Lookalikes

Built from a high-quality source of 1,000+ people when possible. Quality of the seed beats size. A 400-person list of actual depositors beats 20,000 newsletter opens.

- 1% = closest
- 1–3% = scale
- 5–10% = cheap reach, weaker intent

Build lookalikes **per country**. Do not mix US and PA.

### 4. Saved / core audiences (manual)

Age, geo, language, detailed targeting. Still useful for:

- Geo fences: Miami-Dade + Broward + visitors; Panama City + visitors
- Minimum age 21 or 25 for alcohol-forward party creative
- Exclusions

Do not stack 15 interests. That is 2018 media buying.

## Geo rules for this brand

- **Miami demand:** 25-mile radius around Miami Beach / Downtown plus US interest in Miami travel. Separate ad sets or campaigns for locals vs destination visitors if budget allows.
- **Event weeks:** add people recently in Miami (if available) and interest around Art Basel, F1 Miami, Ultra, NYE, boat show.
- **Panama:** Panama City + San Blas interest. Do not bleed Miami creative into Panama targeting.

## Exclusions that should always exist

- Recent purchasers / bookers (7–30 days, then move them to a review / upsell campaign)
- Employees and vendors
- Incomplete junk leads (if you can list them)
- People who already booked the same date window (operational, if CRM can sync)

## First-party data plan

The audience database gets better in this order:

1. Pixel + CAPI on feelingyachty.com (every yacht page, quote, checkout)
2. CRM emails/phones of actual bookers hashed into a custom audience weekly
3. WhatsApp / IG conversation opt-ins where policy allows
4. Lead form field quality (date, headcount, occasion) so offline conversion import is possible
5. Offline conversions: “booked” and “deposit_paid” uploaded so Meta stops optimizing to tire-kickers

Without step 5, Meta will happily buy cheap leads that never charter.
