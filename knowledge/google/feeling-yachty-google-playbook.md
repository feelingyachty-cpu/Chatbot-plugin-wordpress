# Feeling Yachty Google playbook

## Role of Google vs Meta

- **Google Search:** capture people already typing “yacht rental Miami this Saturday.”
- **PMax / Demand Gen / YouTube:** create demand with the same Reels we use on Meta, then let Search close.
- **GA4:** the system of record for on-site behavior.
- **Search Console:** the system of record for organic query demand.
- **Meta:** cheaper visual prospecting and DM/WhatsApp culture.

Do not turn off brand Search because organic ranks. Competitors will bid on the name.

## Campaigns to build

1. Brand Search (exact/phrase, US + relevant geos)
2. Miami non-brand Search (themed ad groups: sunset, wedding, corporate, party, size)
3. Panama Search (separate)
4. Remarketing Display or Demand Gen (site visitors, 30 days)
5. Demand Gen or Video with the best Reels (test, small budget)
6. PMax only after 1–3 have conversion history and customer match

## Landing pages

Use the existing inventory and hubs, not a generic homepage:

- Occasion pages (wedding, corporate, sunset)
- Size / guest-count pages
- Specific yacht URLs when the query is that yacht
- Panama URLs for Panama queries

Title tags and RSA headlines should share language. GSC queries are the copy deck.

## Measurement minimum viable

1. GTM + GA4 on all templates
2. `generate_lead` on quote submit
3. Call / WhatsApp as secondary events
4. `purchase` when deposits exist
5. Enhanced conversions
6. Ads ↔ GA4 link
7. GSC verified + sitemap
8. Offline booked import within 24–48 hours

## Weekly operating cadence

- Monday: GSC queries + GA4 landing pages → content and RSA updates
- Daily: Ads anomalies (disapproved ads, conversion drop, CPA 2×)
- Monthly: Search vs Meta vs organic assisted bookings from CRM
