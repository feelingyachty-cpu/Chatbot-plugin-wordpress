# Feeling Yachty Meta Ads playbook

Brand: luxury yacht charters and event venues. Markets: **Miami** and **Panama**. Site: [feelingyachty.com](https://feelingyachty.com/). Closers happen in WhatsApp, phone, and quote forms — not in a self-serve cart for most large events.

## Business goal

Booked charter days and event buyouts. Not followers. Not cheap leads who want a $99 party boat.

## Funnel

```
Reels / partner content
        ↓
Yacht or occasion page  (ViewContent)
        ↓
Quote / WhatsApp / call  (Lead or Contact)
        ↓
Qualified (date + headcount + budget)
        ↓
Deposit  (Purchase)
```

## Campaign set we should run (once tracking is live)

1. **Miami — Leads — Advantage+**  
   Event: Lead or Schedule. Creatives: sunset, birthday, bachelor/bachelorette, corporate, wedding. Landing: matching occasion or inventory hub.

2. **Miami — Sales — Advantage+** (only after Purchase fires)  
   Event: Purchase / deposit. Retarget ViewContent + Lead.

3. **Miami — Warm retargeting**  
   30–90 day viewers and form abandoners. Higher budget density, shorter Reels, price/proof.

4. **Panama — Awareness + Leads**  
   Separate campaign. San Blas / Panama City creative only. Do not share Miami skyline ads.

5. **Event-week intercept** (Art Basel, F1, Ultra, NYE, Boat Show)  
   Start 4–6 weeks out. Geo + event language. Higher CPA tolerance. Mid-October learning for Q4 holidays.

## Offers that match the existing page inventory

Use real listing language already written for the site, including 110ft Hargrave events, 50–60ft cats, houseboat, mini cruises, ash scattering, proposal, wedding, corporate. Do not invent a yacht that is not bookable.

## Creative backlog (shoot list)

- Captain + safety briefing (corporate trust)
- Wake + skyline 4 seconds, no text
- Table scape / catering (wedding + corporate)
- Water toys + catamaran (group fun)
- Night lighting / DJ deck (party)
- Quiet sunset two-shot (proposal / anniversary)
- Guest-count proof (“this boat holds 49”)
- Karin / Fernando / Captain Alberto as real operators — people book people

## KPIs

| Stage | Target to discuss, not dogma |
| --- | --- |
| Thumbstop (3s) | 25%+ on Reels |
| CTR (link) | 1%+ cold, 2%+ warm |
| Cost / Lead | Set from close rate. If 10% of leads book and a booking is worth $X margin, max CPA = 0.1X |
| Lead quality | Date + headcount + occasion filled |
| Cost / booking | Primary executive number |

LinkedIn today: 85 followers, 29 impressions / 30 days. Meta spend should not wait for LinkedIn to be healthy. They are different machines.

## Do not do

- Boost a post and call it a launch
- Optimize to landing page views on a $20k wedding yacht
- One ad set, one image, $5/day, judged after 36 hours
- Mix Panama and Miami in Advantage+ and hope
- Use stock footage of a yacht we do not operate
