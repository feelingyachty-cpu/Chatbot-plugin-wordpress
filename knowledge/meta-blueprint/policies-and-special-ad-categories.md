# Policies and special ad categories

Official: [Advertising Standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/) · [Business Help](https://www.facebook.com/business/help)

## Why this file exists

Rejected ads waste learning and can restrict the ad account. Luxury nightlife creative sits near several policy edges: alcohol, dating-adjacent bachelor content, before-and-after body imagery, and “get rich / exclusive” claims.

## Special ad categories

If a campaign even *touches* housing, employment, credit, or social issues / elections, Meta forces a special ad category and strips targeting (age, gender, some zips, lookalikes).

Feeling Yachty charters are **not** those categories. Do not accidentally trip them with copy like “hire our crew as jobs” or political event language. Corporate team-building is fine. “Jobs on yachts” is employment.

## Common rejection risks for this brand

| Risk | Do this instead |
| --- | --- |
| Alcohol abuse, binge, minors | Adults, responsible service, no one who looks under 21 drinking |
| Personal attributes (“lonely?”, “fat?”, “broke?”) | Speak to the occasion, not the person’s identity |
| Misleading price (“yachts from $0”) | Show a real starting rate or “request pricing” |
| Before/after body or “party girl” sexualization | Keep it luxury and fun, not adult-adjacen |
| Sensational violence or crash imagery | Safety-positive only |
| Landing page mismatch or broken checkout | Ad URL must work on mobile and match the promise |
| Copyrighted music in ads without rights | Use licensed audio or Meta’s commercial library |
| Fake urgency (“only 1 yacht left” when untrue) | Real inventory scarcity only |

## Restricted: alcohol

Alcohol-related ads have age minimums and country rules. Miami party creative that *shows* drinks is usually acceptable if 21+ targeting / min age is set and we are not selling alcohol as the product. Do not run “open bar included” claims unless the package truly includes it.

## Data and customer lists

Customer lists must be hashed, permissioned, and not purchased third-party junk. Do not upload scraped Instagram followers.

## Account health

- Two-factor on every admin
- No shared logins
- New creatives, not the same rejected image with a crop
- Appeal once with a factual note; do not appeal-spam

If the account is restricted, stop launching new campaigns and fix the flagged asset first.
