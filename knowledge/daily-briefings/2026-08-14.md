### 1. Instagram refreshed its wordmark for the first time in 10 years

- **Source:** TechCrunch — [Instagram introduces a redesigned wordmark](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/13/instagram-introduces-a-redesigned-wordmark/)
- **Also:** [It's Nice That on the design process](https://www.itsnicethat.com/features/behind-instagrams-first-major-refresh-in-10-years-partnership-130826) · [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/tech/instagram-new-logo-update-word-mark-b3033079.html)
- **Summary:** On 13 Aug 2026 Adam Mosseri launched a new Instagram *wordmark* (the script that spells “Instagram”). The rainbow camera app icon is unchanged. Designers kept script instead of going corporate sans-serif so it still feels handmade. Internet joke: the “r” looks like a “z” (“Instagzam”). New fonts and a handwriting typeface shipped with it. Meta would not say whether the app icon is next.
- **Why it matters:** Brand kits, Stories covers, and paid-ad frames that lock to the 2016 script will look dated. This is a culture moment, not an algorithm change.
- **Feeling Yachty:** **Do not rebuild the whole visual identity.** If any template hard-codes the old Instagram script, swap it. A one-frame Reel (“new logo, same water”) is optional, not required. Luxury brands that force a meme about a logo usually look try-hard.

### 2. Meta wants holiday campaigns in learning now, not in November

- **Source:** Social Media Today — [Meta publishes holiday marketing guides](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-publishes-holiday-marketing-guides/827856/)
- **Also:** [Yahoo / Hutchinson on the 34-page guide](https://tech.yahoo.com/social-media/articles/meta-publishes-2026-holiday-planning-173013090.html) · [SMB holiday tips](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-shares-holiday-2026-tips-for-small-businesses/826785/) · [House of Marketers recap](https://houseofmarketers.com/meta-2026-holiday-marketing-guide/)
- **Summary:** Meta’s 2026 holiday playbook (“Winning Hearts and Boosting Carts”) says brands that wait until November already lost. Pillars: Reels as the discovery surface, creator / Partnership ads for trust, Advantage+ for conversion, remove checkout friction. Q2 = find creators, Q3 = test Advantage+ and partnership ads, Q4 = scale. Black Friday is **27 Nov 2026** — a short gap to Christmas. Meta told SMBs to start ads by **mid-October** so the model learns before the spike. Survey points Meta is pushing: 85% of Meta-using shoppers bought in-store after seeing it on social; 59% messaged a business; 94% look to creators; AI shopping is rising.
- **Feeling Yachty:** **NYE, Art Basel, holiday corporate, and warm-weather “escape to Miami/Panama” packages need creative and Advantage+ tests in September–October.** Partnership ads with a real Miami creator or captain-facing handle will beat another polished listing photo. WhatsApp/message ads match the 59% “they will message you” behavior.
