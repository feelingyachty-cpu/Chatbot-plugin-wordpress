# Meta Blueprint certifications (2026)

Source of truth: [Meta for Business Learn](https://www.facebook.com/business/learn) and [Certification](https://www.facebook.com/business/learn/certification). Exams are delivered through Pearson VUE.

## What Blueprint is

Two layers:

1. **Free self-paced courses and live workshops** covering Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, Ads Manager, creative, messaging, and measurement.
2. **Paid proctored exams** that issue a Meta Certified credential.

Courses do not by themselves make you “Meta Certified.” The exam does.

## Exam mechanics (Associate, typical)

- About 60 multiple-choice questions
- About 90 minutes
- Proctored
- Fail wait: commonly 5 days, then full fee again
- You may not sit the exam as “the company.” A named person passes it.

## Associate curriculum Pac-Man has internalized

Mapped from Blueprint Associate prep and Meta Help:

1. **Get started with Meta technologies** — Page, Instagram professional account, WhatsApp Business, Messenger, Meta Business Suite, Business Manager / Business Settings.
2. **Advertising fundamentals** — Why paid distribution exists, auction, total value = bid × estimated action rate × ad quality, ODAX objectives.
3. **Campaign structure** — Campaign (objective, Advantage campaign budget, special ad category) → Ad set (audience, placement, budget if not CBO, optimization event) → Ad (creative, destination).
4. **Objectives (ODAX)** — Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads, App promotion, Sales.
5. **Creative** — Reels-first vertical video, 3–5 primary texts, 3–5 headlines, Advantage+ creative, Partnership ads.
6. **Optimization** — Learning phase, 50 conversions / week as a rule of thumb for stable delivery, do not edit winning ads during learning unless necessary.
7. **Measurement** — Pixel + Conversions API, Event Match Quality, Results / Cost per result / ROAS, Conversion Lift, Experiments.
