# Google Skillshop certifications

Official: [About Google Ads certifications](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9702955)

## Rules

- Free to study and free to take
- Typical assessment: ~50 questions, 75 minutes, **80% to pass**
- Timer cannot be paused. Closing the browser fails the attempt
- Retake after **1 day**
- Valid **12 months**, then retake
- Available in 22 languages
- Individual credential. A coworker being certified does not certify you
- These product certs count toward **Google Partner / Premier Partner** for a company: Search, Display, Video, Shopping ads, Apps
- “Google Ads Professional” style extras do **not** count toward the Partner badge

What you may say while certified: Google recognizes you as a Google Ads certified professional after you passed Skillshop assessments. You may put it on LinkedIn, resume, and site. Partner logos follow Partner usage rules, not the individual cert.

## Product certifications to earn (order for us)

1. **Google Ads Search** — keywords, RSAs, Quality Score, bidding. Core demand capture for “yacht rental Miami.”
2. **Google Ads Measurement** — conversions, enhanced conversions, attribution, GA4 import. Required before we scale.
3. **Google Ads Video** — YouTube / Shorts. Matches our Reels world.
4. **Display** — remarketing and Demand Gen adjacency.
5. **Shopping** — only if a product feed / merchant center becomes real (packages with prices).
6. **Apps** — skip until there is an app.

## GA4 certification

Separate Skillshop path ([intro](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10089681)):

- 101 Get started
- 102 Manage data and read reports
- 201 Deeper reports
- 301 Use Analytics with other tools
- Then the **Google Analytics Certification** exam

## Search Console

There is **no** Skillshop “Search Console Certification” equivalent to Ads. Expertise is demonstrated by using the product and the [Search Console Help](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9128668) + [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs) documentation. This repo is the study guide.
