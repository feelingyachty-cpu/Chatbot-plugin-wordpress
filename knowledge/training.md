# 10 best places to learn social media marketing

These are the locations Pac-Man studies from, and the ones you can enroll in. Ranked for a luxury charter operator who needs organic social, Meta Ads, Google Ads, and analytics — not generic “influencer lifestyle” courses.

## 1. Meta Blueprint (official Meta training)

- **Best for:** Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, Ads Manager, Advantage+, Pixel, CAPI
- **Cost:** Courses free. Digital Marketing Associate exam about $99. Professional exams about $150.
- **Start:** [facebook.com/business/learn](https://www.facebook.com/business/learn)
- **Catalog:** [facebook.com/business/learn/courses](https://www.facebook.com/business/learn/courses)
- **Workshops:** [trainingworkshops.facebookblueprint.com](https://trainingworkshops.facebookblueprint.com/student/catalog)
- **Certifications:** Digital Marketing Associate, Media Buying Professional, Media Planning Professional, Marketing Science Professional, Creative Strategy Professional, Community Manager, Business Marketing Strategy
- **Why this is #1 for us:** This is the source of truth for Meta Ads. The Meta Ads Agent skill is built from this curriculum.

## 2. Google Skillshop (Ads + Analytics)

- **Best for:** Google Ads Search, Display, Video, Shopping, Apps, Measurement, Performance Max, GA4
- **Cost:** Free. Exams free. Valid 12 months. 80% to pass, 75 minutes, 1-day retake wait.
- **Start:** [skillshop.withgoogle.com](https://skillshop.withgoogle.com/)
- **Official cert explainer:** [support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9702955](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9702955)
- **GA4 path:** 101 Get started, 102 Read reports, 201 Deeper reports, 301 Other tools + [Analytics certification](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10089681)
- **Why it is here:** Required for the Google Ads / Analytics / Search Console agent.

## 3. HubSpot Academy — Social Media Marketing Certification

- **Best for:** Strategy, content systems, buyer personas, social analytics, without a paid-ads bias
- **Cost:** Free. About 5 hours. Credential typically valid 2 years.
- **Start:** [academy.hubspot.com](https://academy.hubspot.com/)
- **Course:** [Social Media Marketing Certification](https://academy.hubspot.com/courses/social-media)
- **Why it is here:** Best free “how to think like a social media manager” credential. Pair with Blueprint for paid social.

## 4. Hootsuite Academy

- **Best for:** Day-to-day social operations, calendars, community management, multi-platform workflow
- **Cost:** Platform fundamentals often free. Social Media Marketing Certification commonly around $199. Lifetime credential.
- **Start:** [education.hootsuite.com](https://education.hootsuite.com/)
- **Why it is here:** Closest match to how we actually run Feeling Yachty: comments, DMs, scheduled posts, Slack reporting.

## 5. Meta Social Media Marketing Professional Certificate (Coursera)

- **Best for:** Career-depth program if you want a structured 4–6 month path, including the Associate exam
- **Cost:** Coursera subscription (about $49/month). Audit is free.
- **Start:** [coursera.org/professional-certificates/facebook-social-media-marketing](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/facebook-social-media-marketing)
- **Why it is here:** Official Meta-built sequence. Good if you want homework, projects, and a portfolio — not just help-center reading.

## 6. Google Digital Marketing & E-commerce Certificate (Coursera)

- **Best for:** End-to-end digital: ads, email, analytics, ecommerce measurement
- **Cost:** Coursera subscription. About 6 months if taken as designed.
- **Start:** [coursera.org/professional-certificates/google-digital-marketing-ecommerce](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/google-digital-marketing-ecommerce)
- **Why it is here:** Bridges Google Ads, GA4, and conversion thinking. Useful for Feeling Yachty booking funnels.

## 7. TikTok Academy / TikTok Creative Center

- **Best for:** Short-form video, Spark Ads, Creative Center trends, music, and in-feed ads
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Start:** [ads.tiktok.com/business/learn](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/learn)
- **Creative Center:** [ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter)
- **Why it is here:** Miami yacht content is a Reels/TikTok business whether we like it or not. Official TikTok training beats recycled “viral hacks” blogs.

## 8. LinkedIn Learning — Social Media Marketing path

- **Best for:** LinkedIn organic (our current weakest owned channel), B2B / corporate charter language, employee advocacy
- **Cost:** LinkedIn Premium or Learning subscription.
- **Start:** [linkedin.com/learning](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/)
- **Search:** “Social Media Marketing,” “LinkedIn Marketing,” “Corporate Event Marketing”
- **Why it is here:** Feeling Yachty LinkedIn is at 85 followers and 29 impressions / 30 days. This is the training that fixes that channel.

## 9. Semrush Academy

- **Best for:** Social + SEO + content analytics, keyword-informed posting, competitive research
- **Cost:** Many courses and certificates free with a Semrush account.
- **Start:** [semrush.com/academy](https://www.semrush.com/academy/)
- **Why it is here:** We already plan Miami/Panama blog clusters. Semrush is how social captions, ads keywords, and Search Console queries stay in the same system.

## 10. Digital Marketing Institute — Social Media Specialist

- **Best for:** Formal, exam-backed specialist credential if you want a third-party standard beyond Meta/Google
- **Cost:** Paid program (often $1,000+). Credential typically 3 years.
- **Start:** [digitalmarketinginstitute.com](https://digitalmarketinginstitute.com/)
- **Why it is here:** Highest-rigor generalist social credential. Optional. Do Blueprint + Skillshop + HubSpot first.
