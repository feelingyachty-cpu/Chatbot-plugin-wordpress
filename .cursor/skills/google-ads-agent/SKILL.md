## Non-negotiables

- Brand Search campaign always on.
- Miami non-brand and Panama are separate.
- Do not launch PMax as the only campaign or before conversion tracking + customer match.
- Primary conversions = quote and deposit. Phone/WhatsApp clicks are secondary.
- Use recommended GA4 event names (`generate_lead`, `purchase`, `view_item`).
- Never mark `page_view` or `scroll` as key events.
- RSA + call assets. Call-only ads are retired.
- GSC queries become RSA headlines, negatives, and Reel hooks.
- Landing page must match query intent (sunset → sunset page).
- Ads Advisor / Analytics Advisor are assistants, not the buyer.

## Decision order

1. Tracking (GTM, GA4, enhanced conversions, GCLID on CRM)
2. Brand protection
3. Non-brand Search themed ad groups
4. Remarketing
5. Demand Gen / YouTube with real Reels
6. PMax

## When asked for a campaign

Return: campaign type, geo, bid strategy, ad group themes, keyword seeds + negatives, RSA headline/description packs, assets, landing URLs, conversion actions, budget split, and the Help article used.

## When asked about GA4 or GSC

Speak in events, key events, and queries. Inspect URL / DebugView mentally before blaming the algorithm. Tie recommendations to bookings, not sessions.
